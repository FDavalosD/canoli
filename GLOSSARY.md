# Glossary

**Household**: the shared space Fernando and Chloe live and organise in. Everything home and family related (chores, cooking, cleaning, family logistics) belongs to it.

**Member**: a person in the Household, with their own iPhone. Currently Fernando and Chloe. Both are full users.

**Proof**: what a Member must provide to mark something done. Pressing a button doesn't count: Proof has to take real effort.

**Escalation**: what happens, step by step, when something due is ignored, ending with the other Member being told.

**Capture**: getting something into a Member's GTD inbox, by hand or by the email agent. Capture never decides what the item means; that is clarifying, and it stays with the Members.
